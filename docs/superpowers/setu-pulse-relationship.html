Cognavi · Pulse is the system of record

Where setu lives

setu is the college-facing lens on Pulse. Companies already have a lens (Business Dashboard). The shared graph is College Affinity. Messages and IDs are approved in Pulse, never in a side database.

College door

setu TPO lens

  • Claim office (work email → Pulse collegeContacts)
  • Radar of employers from affinity counts
  • Hiring teams, coordinated hellos, where they studied
  • Reviewed send into Pulse inbox / thread
System of record

Pulse CRM

  • Colleges + display IDs (CLG-…)
  • Companies + JDs + engagements
  • college_employer_affinity — Coresignal counts, both directions
  • collegeContacts — TPO/HOD, email status, portal login
  • Students (2027 batch) + company contacts
  • Staff approve IDs, claims, and data submissions

Affinity is directional intelligence from public profiles — not a verified headcount. Names stay private until the person is on Pulse and replies.

Company door

Business Dashboard company lens

  • Same Pulse company record
  • Sees feeder colleges (affinity PE mode)
  • Gets notified when a campus sends a hello or 2027 profiles
  • Can message back; TPO is notified on setu
