# Pulse Campus voice

Write for a placement officer who needs to know what is visible, what is verified, and what to do next.

- Use direct, plain verbs: Open a company, Choose an alumni seat, Message on Pulse.
- Say "counts" for public-profile affinity and "Alumni on Pulse" only for people who joined Pulse.
- Keep people unnamed until they choose to reply.
- Brief 2027. Describe 2026 as incomplete.
- Never imply a company is pursuing the campus or that Pulse knows a legal identity it does not have.
- Demo controls are labelled as demo tools and remain visually separate from product actions.
